    Class HSMQTTClient

    A connection to an MQTT broker, created by hs.mqtt.create(). The client does nothing until you call connect(). Messages for the topics you subscribe() to arrive as "message" events (see on()). A connected client keeps working after you drop your reference to it, until you call disconnect() or destroy(), or Hammerspoon reloads its configuration. So does a client with listeners registered by on().

      Parameters

      • topicFilter: string

        The topic, which may contain the wildcards + (one level) and # (all remaining levels)

      • Optionalqos: 0 | 2 | 1

        The highest QoS level to receive messages with. Defaults to 0

      Returns Promise<number>

      A Promise resolving to the QoS level the broker granted, which may be lower than requested. It rejects if the broker refuses the subscription
