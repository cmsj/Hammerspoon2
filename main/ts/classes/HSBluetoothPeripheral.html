    Class HSBluetoothPeripheral

    A Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) peripheral, found by hs.bluetooth.scan() or through a paired device's lowEnergyPeripheral(). Obtain peripherals from the hs.bluetooth module; do not instantiate them directly. A peripheral is identified by its identifier, and the same peripheral is returned as the same object for as long as you hold on to it. Call connect() before using its GATT services. A connected peripheral stays connected, and keeps delivering its events, until you call disconnect(), even if you drop your reference to it.

    const [sensor] = await hs.bluetooth.scan({ seconds: 5, services: ["181A"] })
    await sensor.connect()
    const bytes = await sensor.readValue("181A", "2A6E")
    sensor.on("value", (service, characteristic, value) => console.log(characteristic + ": " + value))
    await sensor.subscribe("181A", "2A6E")

    The peripheral's identifier, a UUID that macOS assigns in place of its Bluetooth address, which it doesn't reveal. It is stable on this Mac, but differs between Macs.
