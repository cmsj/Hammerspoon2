HSMQTTClient

Type

A connection to an MQTT broker, created by hs.mqtt.create().

The client does nothing until you call connect(). Messages for the topics you subscribe() to arrive as "message" events (see on()).

A connected client keeps working after you drop your reference to it, until you call disconnect() or destroy(), or Hammerspoon reloads its configuration. So does a client with listeners registered by on().

  • Example:
const client = hs.mqtt.create("mqtt://broker.local")
client.on("message", msg => console.log(msg.topic + ": " + msg.payload))
await client.connect()
await client.subscribe("home/+/temperature")
await client.publish("home/office/light", "on")
